Under instructions from and on behalf of my client, Sri M. Suresh, S/o Sri M. Krishnaiah, residing at Flat 102, Sree Ram Residency, Moghalrajpuram, Vijayawada - 520010, Andhra Pradesh, I hereby serve upon you this formal Legal Notice:

1. PURCHASE TRANSACTION AND CONSIDERATION
My client purchased a Zenith 550-Litre Multi-Door Inverter Refrigerator (Serial No: ZN-2024-REF-88910) on 15th August, 2024 from your authorized branch at Vijayawada against Tax Invoice No. ZE/VIJ/2024/0981 for a valuable consideration of INR 74,990/- (Rupees Seventy-Four Thousand Nine Hundred and Ninety only) paid via credit card transaction. The product carries an unconditional 2-year comprehensive manufacturer warranty and 10-year compressor warranty.

2. INHERENT MANUFACTURING DEFECT
Within 45 days of installation, specifically on 2nd October, 2024, the appliance abruptly ceased cooling, emitting excessive vibration and high-pitched compressor humming noise. The internal digital thermostat displayed error code 'E-04' (Inverter Inverter Board Malfunction), resulting in total spoilage of perishable groceries, dairy produce, and temperature-sensitive diabetes medication valued at INR 8,400/-.

3. DEFICIENCY IN SERVICE AND MULTIPLE FAILED REPAIR ATTEMPTS
My client lodged formal service complaints through your customer toll-free portal:
- Complaint Ticket No. CMS-88102 (dated 03/10/2024): Technician replaced sensor, defect persisted within 48 hours.
- Complaint Ticket No. CMS-89415 (dated 22/10/2024): Technician admitted PCB board defective, requested 14 days for spare parts.
- Complaint Ticket No. CMS-91204 (dated 18/11/2024): New PCB board failed during installation test. Service engineer acknowledged inherent manufacturing defect and recommended total machine replacement.
Despite repeated visits and written follow-ups, your Vijayawada branch has deliberately evaded replacement and refused to process a refund.

4. STATUTORY VIOLATIONS UNDER CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019
The aforesaid acts, omissions, and failure on your part constitute:
(a) Sale of defective goods as defined under Section 2(10) and Section 2(11) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019;
(b) Gross Deficiency in Service under Section 2(11) of the Act;
(c) Unfair Trade Practice under Section 2(47) by false warranty representations.

5. FINAL STATUTORY DEMAND AND 15-DAY DEADLINE
In view of the above facts, you are hereby called upon to comply with the following demands within a strict period of 15 (fifteen) calendar days from the receipt of this legal notice:
(a) Refund the entire invoice amount of INR 74,990/- along with interest at 18% per annum from the date of purchase until realization; OR alternatively deliver a brand new, sealed replacement unit of equivalent or higher model;
(b) Pay compensation of INR 25,000/- for financial loss due to spoiled food/medicines and immense hardship caused;
(c) Pay INR 5,000/- towards legal fees for issuing this statutory notice.

PLEASE NOTE that if you fail to comply with the above demands within the stipulated 15-day deadline, my client has given me strict instructions to institute appropriate legal proceedings against you before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Krishna at Machilipatnam / Vijayawada under Section 35 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, claiming refund, heavy punitive damages of INR 1,00,000/-, litigation costs, and criminal prosecution under Chapter V of the Act, holding you solely responsible for all consequential costs and peril.
