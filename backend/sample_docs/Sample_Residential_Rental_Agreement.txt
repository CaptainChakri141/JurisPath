LESSOR / LANDLORD:
Sri K. Venkata Ramana, S/o Sri K. Satyanarayana, aged about 54 years, residing at Flat No. 402, Sai Balaji Enclave, Lawson's Bay Colony, Visakhapatnam - 530017, Andhra Pradesh (hereinafter called the "LESSOR", which term shall mean and include his heirs, legal representatives, and assigns) of the FIRST PART.

AND

LESSEE / TENANT:
Sri P. Rajesh Kumar, S/o Sri P. Appa Rao, aged about 31 years, employed at SoftWave IT Services, residing currently at D.No. 12-4-15, Seethammadhara, Visakhapatnam - 530013, Andhra Pradesh (hereinafter called the "LESSEE", which term shall mean and include his legal successors) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute and lawful owner of Residential Flat No. 201, Second Floor, measuring 1,450 sq.ft, situated at OceanView Residency, Plot No. 18, Waltair Uplands, Visakhapatnam - 530003, Andhra Pradesh (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHEDULE PREMISES").

AND WHEREAS the Lessee has requested the Lessor to let out the Schedule Premises for residential tenancy and the Lessor has agreed to grant lease on the following mutually agreed terms and conditions:

1. DURATION AND TENURE
The tenancy shall be for a fixed duration of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st October, 2024 and terminating on 31st August, 2025. Any renewal shall be subject to the mutual consent of both parties through a freshly drafted agreement with a standard escalation of 5% in monthly rent.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND PAYMENT DEADLINE
The agreed monthly rent for the Schedule Premises is INR 22,000/- (Rupees Twenty Two Thousand only), exclusive of society maintenance charges and electricity. The Lessee shall remit the rent directly to the Lessor's bank account on or before the 5th (fifth) day of each English calendar month in advance. If rent is delayed beyond the 10th of the month, a late fee penalty of INR 200/- per day shall be levied until full clearance.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Lessee has paid to the Lessor an interest-free refundable Security Deposit of INR 66,000/- (Rupees Sixty-Six Thousand only), equivalent to 3 (three) months' rent, via NEFT transaction ref. UTR-SBI9842104 on 28th September, 2024. The Lessor shall refund the entire security deposit within 7 (seven) business days upon the Lessee peacefully handing over vacant physical possession of the premises, subject to lawful deductions for unpaid utility bills, physical damages, or painting expenses.

4. MAINTENANCE, UTILITIES AND SOCIETY CHARGES
The Lessee shall regularly pay the monthly Apartment Owners Association (RWA) maintenance fee of INR 2,500/- directly to the society office. The Lessee shall bear and promptly pay the actual monthly electricity consumption charges as billed by Andhra Pradesh Eastern Power Distribution Company Limited (APEPDCL) based on independent meter reading.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND TERMINATION NOTICE
Both parties agree to an initial mandatory Lock-in Period of 6 (six) months from the commencement date, during which neither party may terminate this agreement without reasonable cause. After the completion of the 6-month lock-in period:
(a) Either party may terminate this tenancy by providing 2 (two) months prior written notice, or payment of 2 months' rent in lieu of notice.
(b) If the Lessee vacates during the lock-in period, the Lessee shall forfeit one month's security deposit as liquidated damages.
(c) The Lessor reserves the right to terminate the tenancy immediately without notice if the Lessee defaults on rent for two consecutive months or engages in illegal activities.

6. PERMITTED USE AND RESTRICTIONS
The Schedule Premises shall be utilized exclusively for private residential living of the Lessee and his immediate family. Subletting, re-licensing, or assigning possession of any part of the premises to any third party is strictly prohibited without prior written consent of the Lessor. No hazardous, inflammable, or offensive goods shall be stored on the premises.

7. REPAIR, MAINTENANCE AND REPAINTING
The Lessee shall keep the fixtures, fittings, sanitary ware, and electrical points in good tenantable order. Minor day-to-day repairs costing up to INR 1,500/- shall be borne by the Lessee. Major structural repairs shall be addressed by the Lessor. Upon vacating, a standard deduction equivalent to 1 (one) month's rent (INR 22,000) or actual quotation from the security deposit shall be applied towards comprehensive wall repainting and deep sanitation.

8. INSPECTION AND RIGHT OF ENTRY
The Lessor or his authorized agent shall be entitled to enter and inspect the premises during reasonable daylight hours after providing at least 24 (twenty-four) hours prior notice via telephone or messaging to the Lessee.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, including the Andhra Pradesh Residential and Non-Residential Premises Tenancy Act and Indian Contract Act 1872. In the event of any dispute arising out of this agreement, the parties shall first attempt friendly reconciliation. If unresolved within 30 days, the civil courts in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
